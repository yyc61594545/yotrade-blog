## Why your card doesn't work on Jimeng

Jimeng is a mainland-China product. Its checkout is wired to the two domestic wallets and nothing else. Three things stack up against an international user:

1. **Payment.** The checkout accepts WeChat Pay and Alipay only. Since 2023 you can link a foreign Visa or Mastercard to WeChat Pay, but that is aimed at in-person QR payments while travelling; many app and web merchants are excluded from foreign-card wallets, and Jimeng is one of the ones people report failing. It is worth a try if you already have WeChat Pay with a card bound, but do not build a workflow on it.
2. **Account.** Registration wants a mainland +86 phone number or a Douyin account. Virtual and VoIP numbers are usually blocked at signup.
3. **Location.** The web app is happiest with a China or Hong Kong IP; from Europe or the US you may see slow loads or login loops.

The account and location parts have workarounds you have probably already found. Payment is the wall most people hit last, and it is the one this article is about.

### 2. Bind a foreign card to WeChat Pay and try

Install WeChat, go to Me → Services → Wallet → Add a card, and bind a Visa or Mastercard. WeChat will ask for passport verification. Then open the Jimeng checkout on your phone, pick WeChat Pay, and try. If the popup errors out or the button does nothing, you have hit the merchant exclusion and there is no setting to fix it. Cost of trying: fifteen minutes.

## Things that go wrong, and how to avoid them

- **You picked an auto-renew tab.** The popup will look identical, but the QR code is a subscription agreement. Proxy services and friends will (and should) refuse it. Use 单月购买.
- **The QR code expired.** Jimeng's WeChat Pay codes are short-lived. Do not generate the code until you have paid the proxy and are ready to upload.
- **Cropped or blurry screenshot.** The whole QR code has to be visible and sharp; a phone photo of a monitor usually scans, a cropped corner never does.
- **Amount mismatch.** If you ordered Standard but the popup shows ¥649, you clicked the wrong plan. Close it and reopen; do not upload.
- **Refunds after activation.** Once the membership is active, the money is with ByteDance, not with the proxy. Jimeng's own refund policy applies (the plan page advertises 无忧退款, "worry-free refunds", for unused memberships; go through Jimeng's in-app support).
- **Account bans.** Paying by proxy does not touch your login and does not violate the payment terms, but the account itself must be one you obtained legitimately. Shared or purchased accounts are your own risk.
